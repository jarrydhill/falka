Overview

The 8-metre all-day catamaran.

The first FALKA. Eight metres of owner-operated power catamaran with a brief we hadn't seen anywhere else — full-day amenity on a boat you trailer, launch, and walk off. Eight on board, sun-up to sun-down, without anyone eating their lunch off their lap.

Composite hull. Single or twin outboards, F30 through F90. CE Category B offshore certification, a five-year structural and osmosis warranty, and commissioned in Australia before handover. The boat that set the standard — still the one we take out most weekends.

Signature · Wide Stance

Eight metres that feel like ten.

At 4.50 m beam on an 8-metre hull, the FL8 carries room most 10-metre monohulls don't. Walk-around saloon, twin aft sunbeds, a cockpit that holds eight without anyone folding themselves into a corner. The catamaran stance does the work the length doesn't need to.

Build

Composite from stem to stern.

Laid up at a yard we've partnered with for years, commissioned here, and handed over by the same small team that spec'd it. The spec sheet matches the boat. CE Category B offshore certification. Five-year structural and osmosis warranty, written into the contract.

Helm

A boat that doesn't apologise.

Proper galley to port, twin helm seats, Garmin electronics, audio that carries over the engine. Every decision asked one question — does it make a whole day on the water better? If the answer was no, it didn't make it in.
