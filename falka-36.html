Overview

One boat that does all three.

A full day with family and friends. A weekend away. A week along the coast for two. Most boats this size make you choose — day boats run out of amenity by mid-afternoon, and cruisers give up the deck that made the day worth having. The FALKA 36 does both.

Three cabins, two heads, a saloon that stays one open room, and a flybridge you will not want to come down from. Set up so one person can run it, built to order, and commissioned in Australia before handover.

01 / 03 The flybridge

The helm, one level up.

The flybridge carries the main helm, the dining table and the shade. It is where the day actually happens, and the height of eye is worth having when you are reading water on the way into an anchorage.

02 / 03 The saloon

A room on the water.

Accommodation sits in the hulls, so the saloon stays a single open room on the main deck, between the cockpit and the cabins. Two interior schemes, Milano and Baltic — the same joinery drawn in two temperatures.

03 / 03 The cabins

Three cabins, two heads.

Two queen cabins and a bunk cabin, each with hull-side glazing the full length of the berth, and two heads. Room for the week, not just the day.
