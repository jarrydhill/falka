Overview

The three-cabin flybridge.

The FALKA 36 is the larger sister to the FALKA 26: the same wide catamaran stance and the same way of building, carried up to a flybridge and three cabins. A boat for staying out, not just going out.

The helm moves up to the flybridge. Below it, a saloon opens to the cockpit, with a main cabin, a bunk cabin and a bathroom forward. Every one is built to order and commissioned in Australia before handover.
