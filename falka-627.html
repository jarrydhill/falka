Overview

The 6-metre entry to the range.

The smallest FALKA. Six metres of owner-operated power catamaran, trailerable behind a mid-size tow vehicle, launchable on any ramp. Six on board for the whole day, without needing a marina berth or a second mortgage.

Same brief as the rest of the range — sun-up to sun-down, proper shade, real galley — scaled to a first FALKA. Single outboard, 30 to 90 horsepower, composite hull, CE offshore certification. The boat that makes the brand affordable without making it small in the ways that matter.

Signature · Trailerable

Launch anywhere, moor nowhere.

Six metres by 2.75 — the width a standard road trailer allows without permits. Behind a capable SUV or ute, the 627 follows you home at the end of the day. No marina berth, no slipway fees, no second postcode for the boat.

Brief

Small boat, grown-up fit-out.

A compact galley, a proper helm, a composite hardtop that fits under the average garage — not a stripped-back version of the bigger boats. The decisions that matter on the 845 and 1050 are the decisions that matter on the 627. Size came down; standard didn't.

Build

The same yard as the rest of the range.

Laid up by the same partners, commissioned in Australia, handed over by the same small team. CE Category B offshore certification, five-year structural and osmosis warranty, the same specification discipline as the bigger boats. A first FALKA that doesn't feel like a first.
