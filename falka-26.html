Overview

The all-day catamaran.

The first FALKA. Eight metres of owner-operated power catamaran with a brief we hadn't seen anywhere else — full-day amenity on a boat you launch and walk off. Eight on board, sun-up to sun-down, without anyone eating their lunch off their lap.

Composite hull. Single or twin outboards, 30 through 90 horsepower. CE Category B offshore certification, a five-year structural and osmosis warranty, and commissioned in Australia before handover. The boat that set the standard — still the one we take out most weekends.

01 / 03 Wide stance

Eight metres that feel like ten.

At 4.50 m beam on an 8-metre hull, the FL26 carries room most 10-metre monohulls don't. Walk-around saloon, twin aft sunbeds, a cockpit that holds eight without anyone folding themselves into a corner. The catamaran stance does the work the length doesn't need to.

02 / 03 The helm

A boat that doesn't apologise.

Proper galley to port, twin helm seats, Garmin electronics, audio that carries over the engine. Every decision asked one question — does it make a whole day on the water better? If the answer was no, it didn’t make it in.

03 / 03 The deck

Sun-up to sun-down.

Twin aft sunbeds and a cockpit laid out for eight. Full-day amenity on a boat you launch and walk off.
