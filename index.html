The Yard

A quieter way to build.

“The spec sheet matches the boat. The person answering the phone knows your build.”

FALKA isn't a high-volume production yard. Hulls are laid up by a yard we've partnered with for years, commissioned here, and handed over by the same small team that spec'd them.

We make fewer, and we make them properly. It's the only way we're interested in doing it.

Jarryd Hill
Director, Falka Yachtworks
