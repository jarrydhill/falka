Falka Yachtworks

A catamaran for the whole day, not just the morning.

We went looking for a catamaran that could take family and friends from sun-up to sun-down — without tapping out at lunchtime. Everything in the right size was a day boat dressed up. Everything with the right amenity was fifteen metres and a marina berth we didn't want.

So we built the one in the middle. Big-boat amenity on an owner-operated platform. A proper galley. Real shade. Shelter when the weather turns. Room to spread out without being on top of each other.

The Boat

One boat. One standard.

Built to CE Category B offshore certification, backed by a 5-year structural and osmosis warranty, and commissioned in Australia.

Made in Australia

A quieter way to build.

FALKA isn't a high-volume production yard. Hulls are laid up by a yard we've partnered with for years, commissioned here, and handed over by the same small team that spec'd them. The spec sheet matches the boat. The person answering the phone knows your build.

We make fewer, and we make them properly. It's the only way we're interested in doing it.

Jarryd Hill Director, Falka Yachtworks
