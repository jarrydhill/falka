Why We Built It

WE BUILT IT BECAUSE IT DIDN’T EXIST.

I went looking for a cat that could do a full day on the water with family and friends — sun up to sun down — without tapping out at lunchtime. Everything in the right size was a day boat dressed up. Everything with the right amenity was fifteen metres of boat and a marina berth I didn’t want.

So we built the one in the middle. Big-boat amenity in a trailerable, owner-operated platform. Proper galley. Real shade. Shelter when the weather turns. Room to spread out without being on top of each other.

How We Build

MADE THE SLOW WAY. ON PURPOSE.

No production lines. No shortcuts. Every FALKA is hand-laid by a partner yard we’ve worked with, travelled to, and sea-trialled alongside. Our manufacturing spec runs past fifty pages per model — it reads like a legal document because it is one.

Designed here

Naval architecture, systems design, and layout — drawn in Australia, for Australian conditions.

Built by hand

Hulls laid up in the right yard for each model, to a signed spec. No production line.

Lithium-first

Victron Smart BMS, MultiPlus-II, integrated solar. Built for quiet anchor nights, not noisy weekends.

Commissioned here

Every hull arrives home for commissioning, a handover sea trial, and a five-year structural warranty.
