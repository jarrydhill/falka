The Yard

A quieter way to build.

“The spec sheet matches the boat. The person answering the phone knows your build.”

FALKA isn't a high-volume production yard. Hulls are laid up by a yard we've partnered with for years, commissioned here, and handed over by the same small team that spec'd them.

We make fewer, and we make them properly. It's the only way we're interested in doing it.

Jarryd Hill
Director, Falka Yachtworks

Ownership

After the handover.

A FALKA is a long relationship, not a transaction. Service, upgrades, resale, and the unglamorous bits in between — we're the same number you called when you enquired.

Commissioning

Every boat commissioned in Australia before handover. Sea trial, walkthrough, and pre-delivery inspection included.

Servicing

Scheduled service plans, engine checks, and structural audits — through our network or your preferred yard.

Upgrades

Audio, electronics, upholstery, shade. Retrofit what the next season needs without starting again.

Resale

When it's time for the next boat, we help the current owner sell the last one. Short list of buyers, straight answer.
